Privacy Policy — My Sailing Logbook

Last updated: 16 September 2026

My Sailing Logbook ("the app", package name com.ayuus.mysailinglogbook) turns navigation data from your own Actisense W2K-2 device into a sailing logbook on your phone. This page explains what data the app handles and what happens to it.

What data the app collects

The app reads NMEA 2000 navigation data (GPS position, speed, timestamps, and similar boat instrument readings) directly from your own Actisense W2K-2 device over its local Wi‑Fi hotspot. This data comes from your boat's own instruments — the app does not request or use your phone's location, camera, contacts, microphone, or any other device sensor or personal data source, and it requests no such permissions.

Where your data is stored

All logbook data is processed and stored locally on your phone, in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

If you choose to configure optional publishing (to your own WordPress site or your own SFTP server), the login details you enter (WordPress application password, or SFTP host/username/password) are stored on your device using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES‑256 encryption at rest). They are never transmitted anywhere except directly to the server you yourself configured.

What the app shares, and with whom

The app shares data with exactly one destination, and only if you explicitly enable and trigger it: the WordPress site or SFTP server you configure in Settings. Your logbook is sent there directly from your phone. The app developer does not operate, receive data from, or have access to that server.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking libraries. No data is sold or shared with any third party.

Notifications

The app shows a local notification while syncing with the W2K-2 in the background, so you can see progress and know when it's safe to close the app. This uses Android's local/foreground-service notification APIs only — no remote push-notification service is used.

Deleting your data

You can clear the app's cached data at any time from within Settings, or remove all app data by uninstalling the app. This deletes everything the app stored on your device, including any saved publishing credentials.

Children

The app is a sailing logbook tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
